INVESTIGATION NOTES

Incident ID:
INV-001

Initial Classification:
Phishing

Initial Finding:
The affected user received a phishing email containing a
suspicious external link.

User Interaction:
The user clicked the phishing URL.

Endpoint Assessment:
No file download detected.
No suspicious process execution detected.
No malware execution detected.

Authentication Assessment:
Multiple failed login attempts were followed by a successful
login from unfamiliar IP address 185.234.72.91.

Mailbox Assessment:
Unauthorized mailbox access was detected.
An external forwarding rule was created.
Three emails were accessed.
One email containing financial information was accessed.

Impact Assessment:
Potential exposure of sensitive information and unauthorized
mailbox access.

Final Classification:
Likely Account Compromise

Final Severity:
HIGH

Reasoning:
The combination of confirmed phishing activity, user interaction,
suspicious authentication, unauthorized mailbox access, creation
of an external forwarding rule, and access to financial
information strongly indicates a likely account compromise.

Containment:
Account protection initiated.
Suspicious sessions/tokens identified for revocation.
Password reset required.
MFA status verified.
Unauthorized forwarding rule removed.
Further investigation recommended.

Important Limitation:
There is currently insufficient evidence to confirm that
financial information was successfully exfiltrated.

SOC Conclusion:
The incident progressed from a phishing alert to a likely account
compromise. No malware execution was detected on the endpoint.
Further identity, mailbox, and indicator investigation is required.
